Questions

What does the flat rate include?

The full interior design for your space: layout, furniture, lighting, colors, materials and styling, with 3D views, a shopping list and rounds of changes. Furniture, materials and construction are paid separately, straight to the stores and contractors, with no markup from me.

How do you measure square footage?

Only the rooms we're designing. We agree on the number during our first call, and your price is locked in after that.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Recent projects are in Kentucky, New York City and San Francisco. In-person visits can be added (travel at cost).

We're a small startup. Is that too small?

Not at all. I plan office layouts around your next round of hiring, so the space grows with you.

Do you do permit drawings?

Packages cover interior design. If your project needs permits or engineering, I'll work alongside your architect, engineer or contractor; those are quoted separately.

How does paying work?

Half when we start, half at handoff. You always see the full price first.
