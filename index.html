Good questions

Things people ask

What does the flat rate include?

All the design work in your package: the drawings, 3D views, shopping list and revision rounds. Furniture, materials and construction are paid separately, straight to the stores and contractors, and I never add a markup.

How do you measure square footage?

Only the rooms we're designing, not your whole home unless you want the whole thing done. We agree on the number during our first call, and your price is locked in after that.

My project is a little different. Can you still help?

Probably! Say hello and tell me about it. I'll mix packages or give you a custom flat price, still fixed and still up front.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Recent projects are in Kentucky, New York City and San Francisco. Most of the work happens over photos and video calls, and I can visit in person if you'd like (travel at cost).

We're a small startup. Is that too small?

Not at all. I plan office layouts around your next round of hiring, so your space grows with you instead of needing a redo.

Do you do permit drawings?

My packages cover interior design. If your project needs permits or engineering, I'll work alongside your architect, engineer or contractor; those are quoted separately.

How does paying work?

Half when we start, half when I hand everything over. You'll always see the full price first.
