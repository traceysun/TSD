Questions

What does "flat rate" cover?

The design fee for the package you choose: the deliverables and revision rounds listed. Furniture, materials and construction are paid separately, directly to vendors, with no markup from us.

How is square footage measured?

Only the floor area of the spaces we're designing, not your whole home unless you want it all designed. We confirm the number on your intro call and lock the fee before work starts. It doesn't change after that.

What if my project doesn't fit a package?

Book a call. We'll combine packages or quote a custom flat fee, still fixed and still up front.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. TSD works with clients across the US, with recent projects in Kentucky, New York City and San Francisco. Most projects run on photos, measurements and video calls. In-person site visits can be added; travel is billed at cost.

How do office projects handle a growing team?

Office layouts are planned for your next headcount step, so the space flexes as you hire without a redesign.

Do you provide permit drawings?

Packages cover interior design. Where a project needs permits, structural or MEP engineering, TSD coordinates with your architect, engineer or contractor; those services are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

50% when we start, 50% at final handoff. You'll see the full fee before you pay anything.
