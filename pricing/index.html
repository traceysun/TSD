What interior design includes

  • Space planning. A furniture layout that fits how you live or work.
  • Furniture, lighting and decor. Every piece chosen for you, with links and sizes.
  • Colors, materials and finishes. Paint, flooring, stone, fabrics and hardware that work together.
  • 3D views. See the finished room before you order anything.
  • A shopping list. Organized by room, with no markup on anything.
  • Styling notes. Where things go, so the room looks like the design.
