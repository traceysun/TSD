Project 01 · Homeowner · Full Design

Living room with a linear fireplace

The design

The room is organized around a single stone-clad fireplace wall that holds the TV and a linear fireplace, keeping the media and heat in one quiet plane. Slim black wall sconces frame it, and recessed downlights keep the ceiling clean.

A deep wood-lined window brings the garden in, softened by a sheer roller shade, while pale oak floors, a textured wool rug and a low walnut table keep the palette warm and restrained.
