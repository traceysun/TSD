A Quiet Living Room

Project 01 · Home · Living room

Everything leans on one quiet stone wall. The TV and a long, low fireplace share it, so screen and fire sit in the same calm spot. Two slim black sconces frame it, and small recessed lights keep the ceiling clean.

The wood-lined window is the real star: in the afternoon, leaves outside throw shadows across the whole room. Pale oak floors, a wool rug and a low walnut table keep it warm.
