Project 01 · Home · Full Room

A calm living room with a linear fireplace

The story

Everything here leans on one quiet stone wall. The TV and a long, low fireplace share it, so the screen and the fire live in the same calm spot instead of fighting for attention. Two slim black sconces frame it, and tiny recessed lights keep the ceiling clean.

The big wood-lined window is the real star: in the afternoon the leaves outside throw shadows across the whole room. Pale oak floors, a chunky wool rug and a low walnut table keep it warm and easy.
