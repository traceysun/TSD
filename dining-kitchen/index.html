A Kitchen for Gathering

Project 03 · Home · Kitchen, dining & living

This one is all about a long, open room that does three jobs at once. One wall of tall, handle-free white cabinets hides the fridge and pantry and holds two built-in ovens, with a row of pale oak uppers and a stone counter for everyday cooking.

In the middle, an oval walnut table on a woven rug makes the dining spot, with a mix of oxblood and black leather chairs. Past it, an olive sofa, a rust lounge chair and a paper lantern make a cozy corner by the windows. A walnut console by the door catches keys.
