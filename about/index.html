About

I’m Tracey.

I trained in architecture before working in interiors. I still look at a room the way I look at a floor plan. Where does the light come in? What do you walk past to get to the window?

Once the layout makes sense, we can pick furniture, work out the lighting, and see how it all looks together in 3D.

I work with homeowners and startup teams across the US.

How I work

  1. 01

    First call

    We’ll spend 20 minutes talking about how you use the space, what you’d change, and your budget. The call is free.

  2. 02

    Measure

    You can send me photos and a few measurements, or I can come measure.

  3. 03

    Design review

    I’ll show you the layout, furniture, lighting and materials in 3D so we can make changes together.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    I send the shopping list, drawings and styling notes, and stay available for questions.
