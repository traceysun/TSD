About

I'm Tracey.

I trained in architecture before working in interiors. I start with the layout and the light, then work out what furniture, lighting and finishes belong in the room.

I work with homeowners and startup teams across the US.

How I work

  1. 01

    First call

    A free 20-minute call about your space, how you use it and your budget.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Send photos and a few measurements, or I'll come measure.

  3. 03

    Design review

    Review the layout, furniture, lighting and materials in 3D before we finalize the design.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    I send the shopping list, drawings and styling notes, and stay available for questions.
